For researchers

Make your research data FAIR.

Discover and reuse materials science data, organize and share your research data, and publish your projects with a DOI. Get started with the NOMAD Central Platform, free and open to the research community.

Why researchers use NOMAD

Discover, manage, and share research data

NOMAD helps researchers discover and reuse existing data, organize and collaborate on research projects, and publish FAIR, citable results.

Research workflows

Choose a route into NOMAD

Explore public data without an account, or sign in to organize, share, and publish your own research.

travel_explore

Explore & reuse data

Search published computational and experimental data using structured scientific metadata.

  • Explore 19M+ entries without an account
  • Inspect processed results and their original research files
  • Download data for comparison, validation, or further analysis
No account required
Open NOMAD Central →
folder_shared

Organize & collaborate

Bring related files, structured entries, project information, and collaborators together.

  • Create a project for a study, publication, or shared workflow
  • Keep files private while work is in progress
  • Invite coauthors with edit access or reviewers with read access
Free account
Follow the project tutorial →
verified

Publish & cite

Make a completed project findable and reusable while keeping control of when data become public.

  • Review the files and entries before irreversible publication
  • Publish immediately or use an embargo
  • Assign a DOI to a published project on NOMAD Central Platform
