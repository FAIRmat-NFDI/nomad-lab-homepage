Related projects
NOMAD is developed and used by other projects. These projects share common goals and values. Many team members are active in these related projects as well.

The content here is carried over unchanged from the old site — the same three partner projects, with the same copy. If the ecosystem has grown since, or these descriptions are out of date, this is the page to expand.

FAIRmat

FAIRmat is an NFDI consortium that is building a federated data infrastructure for solid state physics in Germany and beyond.

  • The main NOMAD developer
  • Extends NOMAD beyond theory
  • A federated data infrastructure
FAIRmat homepage →

NOMAD CoE

The NOMAD Center of Excellence is a European Union Horizon 2020 project on using exascale computing for computational materials science.

  • Uses NOMAD to manage data
  • Analytics on exascale hardware
  • NOMAD for extreme-scale data
NOMAD CoE homepage →

FAIR-DI e.V.

FAIR-DI is a multi-national association to proliferate FAIR data practices in physics, chemistry, materials science, and astronomy.

  • Hosts many related events
  • Connects FAIR data projects
  • Brings our ideas to other fields
FAIR-DI homepage →

Open infrastructure for scientific data.

Developed by FAIRmat within Germany's National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI).
