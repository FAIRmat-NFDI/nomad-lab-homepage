For research groups · Networks · Institutions · IT

A research data platform for your group, network, or institution.

Manage your organization's research data on your own infrastructure with NOMAD Oasis. Open source, developed by FAIRmat within Germany's NFDI initiative, and used by research groups, network projects, and institutions across Europe and beyond.

Used by 90+ institutions worldwide
Why organizations choose NOMAD
Three reasons to adopt NOMAD Oasis

Keep control of research data, establish consistent practices across projects, and build on an open ecosystem instead of starting from scratch.

Data sovereignty

Keep data under your control

Run NOMAD Oasis on infrastructure you operate, integrate institutional storage and identity services, and choose what to publish to NOMAD Central.

  • Self-hosted deployment
  • Institutional storage and identity integration
  • Your access and data policies
  • Selective public publishing
Consistent data practices

Structured & reusable data

Use shared and customizable schemas to manage structured, machine-actionable data consistently across projects and support FAIR data practices.

  • Shared and customizable schemas
  • Machine-actionable metadata
  • Consistent data capture and processing
  • Support for funder expectations
Open ecosystem

Start from a shared foundation

Reuse FAIRmat- and community-developed plugins, templates, tools, and integrations, then adapt NOMAD to your domain and institutional workflows.

  • Reusable plugins and templates
  • Domain-specific customization
  • Integration with research infrastructure
  • Open source under Apache 2.0
Who this is for
One platform, three scales of organization
NOMAD can be adapted to the needs of an individual research group, a research organization, or a multi-institution network, supporting different requirements for data control, collaboration, and integration.
groups

Single research groups

Give your lab a shared environment to collect, organize, analyze, and prepare research data for publication.

apartment

Research organizations

Provide teams across your institution with shared infrastructure connected to existing storage, identity services, and policies.

hub

Multi-institution networks

Coordinate data across partner institutions through shared schemas, workflows, and controlled data exchange.

Governance & credibility
Why NOMAD is a credible choice

NOMAD combines transparent public governance, demonstrated research uptake, and more than a decade of open development.

Governance

Developed by FAIRmat

FAIRmat is a consortium of German research institutions operating under the NFDI framework. Publicly funded and transparently governed.

Citations & reuse

Cited in 213+ papers

The NOMAD platform paper has 213 citations, and around 656 publications reference a NOMAD-minted DOI in their data availability statement. Read from Google Scholar in July 2026.

Track record

Actively developed since 2014

Over a decade of continuous development, backed by recurring EU and German federal funding. Fully open source under Apache 2.0.

Part of the wider research data ecosystem

NOMAD does not operate in isolation. It connects with the broader European and German research data infrastructure.

DE

NFDI

NOMAD is developed by FAIRmat as part of Germany's National Research Data Infrastructure, connecting domain communities and shared services.

EU

EOSC Node Germany

NOMAD is one of the use cases helping connect German research data services with the European Open Science Cloud.

EU

MaterialsCommons

NOMAD is one of the initial infrastructures being integrated into MaterialsCommons, a federated European ecosystem for materials data, tools, and workflows.

Deployment options
Two paths to adoption

Run NOMAD Oasis directly with your own team, or work with a managed deployment partner. Both paths keep you on the same open technology base.

Self-serve

Run it yourself

Use the open-source NOMAD Oasis stack and deploy on your own infrastructure with full operational control.

  • Full NOMAD Oasis codebase
  • Docker Compose or Kubernetes deployment
  • Public installation guides and docs
  • Integrate with your infrastructure and identity stack
  • End-to-end control of operations and governance
Managed

Professional services

Partner with Glaide Data for managed deployment and adoption support based on the NOMAD Oasis platform.

  • Everything included in self-serve
  • Technical onboarding and deployment planning
  • Schema and plugin customization support
  • Hosting and operations support options
  • Practical integration support for institutional workflows
