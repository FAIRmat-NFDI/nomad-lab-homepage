FOR RESEARCH GROUPS · NETWORKS · INSTITUTIONS · IT
A research data platform for your group, network, or institution.

Manage your organization's research data on your own infrastructure with NOMAD Oasis. Open source, developed by FAIRmat within Germany's NFDI initiative, and used by research groups, network projects, and institutions across Europe and beyond.

[ institutional hero visual ]
Why organizations choose NOMAD
Three reasons to adopt

Whether you are a single research group, a network project, or a full institution, these are the concerns that come up at an organizational level.

Data sovereignty

Run on your own infrastructure

Your data stays on your infrastructure. Run Oasis behind your firewall, use your own storage, and implement your own access policies.

  • Self-hosted deployment
  • Your existing storage and auth
  • Institutional data policies apply
  • No vendor lock-in
FAIR by default

Compliant and reusable data

NOMAD enforces FAIR metadata at schema level and supports funder requirements across DFG, NFDI, and Horizon projects.

  • Machine-actionable metadata
  • Supports DFG, Horizon, NFDI requirements
  • Exportable to public NOMAD
Publicly funded and open

Sustainable and transparent

NOMAD is developed by FAIRmat within Germany's NFDI initiative and released as open source with transparent governance.

  • Developed by FAIRmat consortium
  • Part of NFDI (Germany)
  • Open source, Apache 2.0
  • Actively developed since 2014

Who this is for
One platform, three scales of organization
NOMAD adapts to your structure from a single lab to a national network. Each comes with different data sovereignty, sharing, and integration needs.
groups

Single research groups

One PI, a handful of researchers, shared instruments and codes. Needs a private space to manage and pre-publish data.

apartment

Research organizations

Universities, institutes, and national labs with governance and identity integration needs.

hub

Multi-institution networks

Collaborative Research Centres, NFDI consortia, and EU projects sharing schemas and data across partners.

Governance and credibility
Why NOMAD is a credible choice

Three concerns institutions face when evaluating adoption, and how NOMAD addresses each.

Governance

Developed by FAIRmat

FAIRmat is a consortium of German research institutions operating under the NFDI framework. Publicly funded and transparently governed.

Peer-reviewed

Cited in 213+ papers

The NOMAD platform paper has 213 citations, and around 656 publications reference a NOMAD-minted DOI in their data availability statement. Read from Google Scholar in July 2026.

Track record

Actively developed since 2014

Over a decade of continuous development, backed by recurring EU and German federal funding. Fully open source under Apache 2.0.

Part of the wider research data ecosystem

NOMAD does not operate in isolation. It connects with the broader European and German research data infrastructure.

EU

EOSC

European Open Science Cloud. NOMAD is a registered service in the EOSC marketplace.

DE

NFDI Basic Services

Connected to NFDI's basic services and data discovery infrastructure.

Federation

Re3data and FAIRsharing

Registered in major repository directories with persistent identifiers.

Deployment options
Two paths to adoption

Run NOMAD Oasis directly with your own team, or work with a managed deployment partner. Both paths keep you on the same open technology base.

Self-serve

Run it yourself

Use the open-source NOMAD Oasis stack and deploy on your own infrastructure with full operational control.

  • Full NOMAD Oasis codebase
  • Docker Compose or Kubernetes deployment
  • Public installation guides and docs
  • Integrate with your infrastructure and identity stack
  • End-to-end control of operations and governance
Managed

Professional services

Partner with Glaide Data for managed deployment and adoption support based on the NOMAD Oasis platform.

  • Everything included in self-serve
  • Technical onboarding and deployment planning
  • Schema and plugin customization support
  • Hosting and operations support options
  • Practical integration support for institutional workflows
