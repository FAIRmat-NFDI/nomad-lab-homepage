Features
What NOMAD Central gives you
A free web service that lets you share your data or use comprehensive data that others provide. Organize, analyze, share, and publish your materials science data, and explore your colleagues' data in the same place.
cloud_upload
Drag & drop uploads

Upload files individually or zip entire directory structures. NOMAD organizes them the way you would on your hard drive.

link
Free DOI assignment

Publish and archive your data for free. Assign DOIs to datasets and reference them directly in your papers.

shield
You control your data

Not ready to publish? Work in private first, collaborate with your group, or switch to Oasis for higher privacy.

code
FAIR by default

Machine-actionable data extracted from 60+ codes and formats. Findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable.

storage
One source of truth

The largest open materials database of its kind, combining data from Materials Project, AFLOW, OQMD, and more.

play_arrow
Ready-to-use tools

Run Jupyter notebooks and analysis tools directly on NOMAD. Access and publish notebooks alongside your data.

Benefits
Why NOMAD is different
NOMAD is not about sharing files, it is about sharing data and making data FAIR. All data is organized in well-defined structures described by a formal schema. That schema is what enables search, APIs, interoperability, and analysis.

Structured data and Metainfo

All data in NOMAD follows a schema that defines how data is hierarchically structured and cross-referenced. This schema is called the NOMAD Metainfo. It covers general domain-independent structures and detailed, method-specific content.

  • NOMAD parsers automatically structure your data
  • You can extend the existing NOMAD schema
  • All data is hierarchically organized and connected

Analysis tools (NORTH)

Because all data is uniformly organized, you can use existing tools or build your own to analyze data from any source. The NOMAD Remote Tools Hub (NORTH) runs containerized tools and Jupyter notebooks directly on your data.

  • Use JupyterLab to access and modify files in NOMAD
  • Run predefined notebooks for ML in the AI Toolkit
  • All data is available via API

Who builds it
Developed by FAIRmat

FAIRmat develops and operates NOMAD as part of a federated data infrastructure for materials science. It is the consortium that backs NOMAD as part of Germany's NFDI initiative.
