Core Platform

NOMAD Central

The flagship service. Free, open, and ready to use. Explore and reuse data from 19M+ entries, upload your own data, publish with a DOI. No installation required.

What NOMAD offers
manage_search
Search apps

Tailored search interfaces for domains across simulation and experiment.

data_object
Automated Parsing

Parse and structure raw simulation and instrument outputs for 60+ formats.

article
Electronic lab notebooks

Capture experiments as structured, FAIR data, right at the bench.

workspace_premium
Project DOIs

Publish your project with a persistant, citable DOI.

terminal
Remote tools HUB

Analyze repository data using Jupyter notebooks and other containerized tools.

Workflows

Link data entries into traceable workflows.

Actions

Run predefined executable workflows, from ML export to model training.

dashboard
Dashboards

Build custom dashboards for your data and workflows.

Run your own NOMAD instance. Your resources, your rules.

  • Control access and keep data on your infrastructure
  • Customize for your research workflows
  • Publish to NOMAD Central from your Oasis
